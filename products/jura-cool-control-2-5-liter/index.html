Jura Cool Control 2.5 Liter

Coffee creations with milk and milk foam are widely popular. For perfect results, they need milk that is chilled to 39 °F. The Cool Control with its dishwasher-safe stainless steel milk container is really easy to fill and keeps the milk at the ideal temperature for the whole day. Thanks to its large holding capacity of 85 oz and locking mechanism included as standard, the cooler is ideally suited to the requirements of any professional setting.
