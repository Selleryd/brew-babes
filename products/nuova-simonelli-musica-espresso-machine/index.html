Drink recipes and preparation

Barista-prepared recipe possibilities. Grinding, dosing, extraction and milk preparation require the operator; these are not one-touch programs.

Espresso

Grind, dose and tamp separately; brew through the portafilter.

Ristretto

A shorter espresso recipe set through grind, dose and yield.

Double espresso

Use the appropriate basket, dose and brew recipe.

Cappuccino

Espresso plus milk textured in a pitcher and poured by the operator.

Caffè latte

Espresso with a larger portion of steamed milk, assembled by the operator.

Flat white

A barista recipe using espresso and finely textured milk; the operator controls the proportions.

Espresso macchiato

Espresso finished with a small amount of textured milk by the operator.

Iced latte

Manually combine espresso with cold milk and ice. This is not cold extraction.

Mocha

Manually add chocolate to espresso and textured milk; chocolate is a separate ingredient.

Americano

Combine espresso with hot water; the operator sets the proportions.

Hot water

For tea or infusions; tea itself is added separately.

Preparation and configuration notes

  • Drink cards are manually assembled recipe possibilities, not one-touch programs or a fixed factory menu. A separate espresso grinder, milk pitcher and suitable coffee are required.
  • The seller lists only a Black variant without identifying water connection. Confirm tank versus plumbed configuration; do not infer it from the finish.
